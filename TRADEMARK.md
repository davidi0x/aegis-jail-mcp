# Aegis Jail MCP trademark policy

The **Aegis Jail MCP** name (and its short form, **Aegis**) and the shield logo (`.claude-plugin/icon.png`) are trademarks
of the project owner (davidi0x). The AGPL-3.0 license covers the *code*; it does
not license the *name* or the *logo*. This is normal for open-source projects
(Python, Firefox, Rust and many others do the same) and it is what lets us keep
"Aegis" meaning *this* project.

## You may, without asking
- Use, modify and redistribute the software under the AGPL.
- Say your product "works with Aegis", "is based on Aegis" or "is a fork of Aegis",
  as long as it is truthful and does not imply we endorse or produce it.
- Keep the name in unmodified copies you redistribute.

## Please do not, without written permission
- Use the name "Aegis" or the logo as the name/brand of **your** product, service,
  company or a modified distribution (rename your fork).
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests official status, endorsement or
  affiliation.
- Register "Aegis" (or a confusingly similar name) as a package, domain, social
  account or trademark in this field.

## Why
Aegis is a security tool. If anyone could ship a modified build under the name
"Aegis", users could no longer trust that something called Aegis behaves the way
the audited project does. Protecting the name protects the people who rely on it.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue or contact the owner.
